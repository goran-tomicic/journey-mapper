Interview subject: Priya, Operations Lead at a mid-size logistics company
Topic: Onboarding onto a new B2B SaaS platform

We signed up after a demo call, and honestly the first login was rough. Nobody told us we needed to pre-configure our user roles before inviting the rest of the team, so I invited twelve people and half of them landed on a blank dashboard with no permissions. That was frustrating — I remember thinking "did we just buy the wrong tool?"

Once support pointed me to the roles page, setup itself was actually pretty painless. The step-by-step wizard for connecting our existing data source worked first try, which was a relief after the rocky start.

The part that really won me over was the first time I saw our shipment data populate the dashboard automatically. I actually said out loud, "oh, that's slick" — my team was impressed too. It felt like the tool finally showed its value.

Where it fell apart again was integrating with our accounting software. The docs referenced an API version that didn't match what was actually live, so I burned almost a full day going back and forth with support before we got it working. I was pretty annoyed by that point, like, how is this not in the setup wizard?

By the end of the first month we were fully running on it and honestly happy, but that first week nearly made us churn.
